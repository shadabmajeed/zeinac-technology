About Us

Zeinac Technologies is a Ranchi-based technology and supply company delivering reliable IT hardware, security systems, and office solutions to government bodies, corporates, and institutions across Jharkhand and beyond.

We specialize in end-to-end procurement and support — from supplying laptops, servers and CCTV systems to providing Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) and on-site IT support. With strong experience in GeM and tender-based supply, we make institutional purchasing simple, compliant, and dependable.

Government & GeM Supply Capabilities

Zeinac Technologies is well-versed in public procurement. We supply through the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) and participate in open tenders, ensuring transparent, compliant, and timely delivery for government departments and institutions.

  • Registered GeM seller for IT & office products
  • Tender participation & bid management
  • Documentation, warranty & compliance support
  • On-time bulk delivery & after-sales service
